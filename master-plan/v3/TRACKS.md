| **Main risk** | Luminal access and limits; a flat curve; a toy problem | No buyer in the room; whether attendees count as customers ⚠; payment method ⚠ |
| **Prizes it stacks with** | MongoDB ($1k credits), AGI ($500), Luminal credits | MongoDB, AWS, Bloomerang; possibly AGI |

**Verdict:** Track 2 fits this team and offers roughly twice the prize money. The only thing that can sink it is having no buyer, which is a sales problem, and selling is the team's strength. Track 1 is the backup only if the team turns out to be all engineers with no seller.
